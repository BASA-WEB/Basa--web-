BASA Takes Campaign to Ibarapa Central

Hon. Bolanle Aminat Sarumi-Aliyu (BASA), PDP Senatorial Candidate for Oyo South, today took her campaign and message of people-centred representation to Ibarapa Central Local Government Area.

BASA engaging with residents during the Ibarapa Central campaign.
BASA connecting directly with people during the market rally.

The grassroots engagement, held during BASA's Townbowo Market Rally in Ibarapa Central, brought the candidate closer to residents, traders, supporters, community members and other stakeholders.

During the visit, BASA interacted directly with people in the market and surrounding community, listening to their concerns and sharing her vision for a better and more inclusive Oyo South.

People First. Progress Always.

The visit reflects BASA's continued commitment to taking her campaign beyond political meetings and into the communities where people live, work and conduct their daily activities.

As the campaign continues across Oyo South, BASA is reaffirming her commitment to effective representation, economic opportunity, youth empowerment, support for women, community development and responsive leadership.

The Ibarapa Central engagement is part of BASA's ongoing grassroots campaign as she continues to connect with the people across the nine local government areas of Oyo South.
