About the Competition

OYO GOT SPIN 2026 is a statewide DJ talent competition designed to discover, showcase and promote talented DJs across all parts of Oyo State.

The competition is open to all eligible contestants in Oyo State, and it is built to spotlight skill, creativity, performance, and originality.

Competition Rules & Eligibility

  • Contestants must have a valid Oyo State Voter's Card (PVC).
  • The PVC also serves as proof of residency in Oyo State.
  • Entry opens September 4th – 25th, 2026 at the BASA Campaign Office, Molete, Baba Olunloyo House.
  • Contestants must submit a DJ performance video that is no more than 2 minutes long.
  • Audition videos must be sent via WhatsApp after completing online registration.
  • Judging will be based on skill, creativity, performance, and originality.
  • Only one entry per contestant is allowed.
  • The decision of the judges is final.
  • The Grand Prize winner will receive ₦1,000,000 cash.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can enter OYO GOT SPIN 2026?

Any DJ resident in Oyo State with a valid Oyo State Voter's Card can enter. The card also serves as proof of residency.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Registration for OYO GOT SPIN 2026 is completely free.

How do I submit my audition video?

After completing the online registration form, record a DJ performance video of no more than 2 minutes and send it via WhatsApp to 0814 803 5104.

What if I don't have a PVC?

A valid Oyo State Voter's Card is required to enter, as it also serves as proof of residency in the state.

When does registration close?

Entries open from September 4th to September 25th, 2026 at the BASA Campaign Office, Molete, Baba Olunloyo House.

What can I win?

The overall winner receives a grand prize of ₦1,000,000 cash.
