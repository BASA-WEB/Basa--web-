About the Competition

OYO GOT SPIN 2026 is a statewide DJ talent hunt created to discover, promote and celebrate talented DJs across Oyo State.

Contestants from all 33 Local Government Areas of Oyo State are welcome to take part. Whether you play at street parties, weddings, clubs or from your bedroom setup, OYO GOT SPIN gives you a fair, structured platform to show what you can do and be recognised for it. Register below, then send your audition video on WhatsApp to enter the running.

🎥 Audition Video Requirement

  • Record a DJ performance video of not more than 2 minutes.
  • Complete the registration form below first.
  • After registering, send your audition video through WhatsApp — not through this website.

We do not accept video uploads on this site. All audition videos are submitted directly to our WhatsApp line so we can review them quickly.

Competition Rules

  1. OYO GOT SPIN 2026 is open to eligible DJs across Oyo State.
  2. Participants must provide accurate registration information.
  3. Participants must provide a valid PVC Number.
  4. Each participant must submit an original DJ performance video.
  5. The audition video must not exceed 2 minutes.
  6. Audition videos must be submitted through WhatsApp.
  7. Participants must follow all official competition rules.
  8. The organizers reserve the right to disqualify entries that violate the rules.
