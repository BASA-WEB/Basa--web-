About the Competition

OYO GOT SPIN 2026 is a statewide DJ talent competition designed to discover, showcase and promote talented DJs across all parts of Oyo State.

Competition Rules

  • 1. The competition is open to eligible DJs across Oyo State.

  • 4. Contestants must submit an original DJ performance video.
